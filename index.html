About National Benefits USA

Connecting Families to What Matters Most

At National Benefits USA, we believe that reliable internet access is no longer a luxury—it’s a necessity. From education and work to healthcare and staying in touch with loved ones, the internet has become the backbone of modern life. Yet, too many households are left behind simply because of cost.

Our mission is simple: to bridge that gap. Through community support and partnerships, we help qualified families access high-speed internet services at no cost for six months. No hidden fees. No long-term contracts. Just the connection you need to stay ahead.
