About National Benefits USA

We Help Deserving Families Get Connected

At National Benefits USA, we believe everyone deserves access to reliable internet. But we also know that navigating government programs like Lifeline and the Survivor Benefit can be confusing and overwhelming. Our mission is to change that.

We act as your guide. We help you understand if you qualify for programs like the Survivor Benefit (which supports survivors of domestic violence) or Lifeline. We simplify the application steps, making the process straightforward and stress-free. And for families who don’t qualify for government aid, our community-funded program can still provide 6 months of free internet.

Where Our Support Comes From

National Benefits USA is supported by two main sources: government programs like Lifeline and the Survivor Benefit, and generous community donations. This allows us to help as many families as possible.

Lifeline & Survivor Benefit

Through the federal Lifeline program and the Survivor Benefit (part of the Safe Connections Act), eligible households can receive a discount on phone and internet service. We help you navigate the application process.
